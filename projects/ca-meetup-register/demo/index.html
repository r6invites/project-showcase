A two-minute tour

Follow these in order to see a registration travel all the way through.

  1. Register. On the public page press Register, enter any name and a 10-digit number such as 9000012345, and upload any picture as the payment screenshot.
  2. Approve. In the dashboard open Payments. Your booking is at the top. Press Paid, then Yes, approve. Ticket numbers are issued.
  3. Get the ticket. Back on the public page choose View my ticket, enter your Booking ID and number, and open the ticket. Download it as JPG or PDF.
  4. Admit. In the gate view, search your name and admit yourself, or scan one of the sample QR codes below. Scan it twice to see the “already scanned” warning.
  5. Check payments automatically. In Payments press Scan screenshots. The page reads the payment ID and amount from each screenshot and flags two bookings that share one ID.
  6. Look around. The Today page summarises what needs attention. People, Feedback, Game messages and Settings are all live.

What is inside

  • Public registration with group bookings, a spots counter and a closing time
  • Payment by UPI with a screenshot upload
  • Ticket generation as a picture and PDF, with a QR on every ticket
  • QR scanning at the gate with the camera, and a search for anyone without a code
  • Payment-screenshot reading (OCR) that finds payment IDs and amounts and flags repeats
  • Dashboard summary with charts, pending payments and live counts
  • Ticket transfers between participants, with admin approval
  • Warnings for many sign-ups from one network and for reused payment IDs
  • Feedback, game messages and prizes handed out per ticket
  • Settings with a change log for price, limit, deadline, contacts and event details
Demo only. Every name, phone number, payment and screenshot here is made up. Nothing is sent anywhere. The data lives only in this browser, so what you add stays on your device and other visitors never see it. The look of the original event is kept so you can see the real design.
